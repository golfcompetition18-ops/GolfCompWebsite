TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

A fair game starts with clear rules.

01 / From Index to strokes

18-hole Course Handicap = Handicap Index × Slope ÷ 113 + Course Rating − Par.

Full precision carries into the allowance calculation. The final Playing Handicap is rounded to a whole number; exact halves round upwards, including +1.5 to +1.

Enter plus indices as negative values, e.g. −2.4 for +2.4.

02 / Scoring your format

Stroke play: lowest total net wins. Stableford: 2 points for net par, 1 for bogey, 3 for birdie, and zero for net double bogey or worse.

Fourball points uses the better partner’s points per hole. Match play compares net hole scores and ends when a lead exceeds holes remaining. Teams A and B must have two players each for fourball.

03 / Tees & stroke allocation

Shots fall on stroke indices 1 upwards, repeating after 18. Plus Playing Handicaps give strokes back from index 18 downwards.

Absolute net stroke and match formats add the difference from the lowest tee par after allowance. Stableford uses each player’s own par without that adjustment. Match strokes are calculated relative to the lowest player before final rounding.

04 / Know the boundary

This is a social competition scorer with an England-oriented WHS calculation profile. It does not issue official indices, submit scores, calculate PCC or expected scores, certify cards, or adjudicate penalties and Local Rules.

Gross entries must already include applicable penalties. A picked-up stroke-play hole is a no return. Blank holes remain unplayed. Match X means no valid hole score, not a concession. Ties remain tied; agree play-off terms separately.
